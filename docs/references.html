References

All literature cited in or directly relevant to the NAS-PINNs thesis project. Organised by topic.

Primary Work
[1]
Mortensen, Noorsumar, Fjær, Babaei, Drønen — Neural Architecture Search for Physics-Informed Neural Networks in FEM Acceleration
International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology · 2026 · DOI: 10.1007/s00170-026-17515-w
Primary reference
PINN Foundations
[2]
Raissi, M., Perdikaris, P., Karniadakis, G.E. — Physics-informed neural networks: A deep learning framework for solving forward and inverse problems involving nonlinear PDEs
Journal of Computational Physics · vol. 378 · pp. 686–707 · 2019
The foundational PINN paper. Introduces the loss decomposition L = L_pde + L_bc + L_ic and demonstrates application to Burgers, Schrödinger, and Navier–Stokes equations.
PINN foundation
[3]
Lagaris, I.E., Likas, A., Fotiadis, D.I. — Artificial neural networks for solving ordinary and partial differential equations
IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks · vol. 9, no. 5 · pp. 987–1000 · 1998
Precursor to PINNs — first systematic use of NNs as PDE solvers by encoding BCs into the network ansatz.
Early physics-NN
[4]
Karniadakis, G.E., Kevrekidis, I.G., Lu, L., Perdikaris, P., Wang, S., Yang, L. — Physics-informed machine learning
Nature Reviews Physics · vol. 3 · pp. 422–440 · 2021
Comprehensive review of physics-constrained ML methods, covering PINNs, DeepONet, and hybrid approaches. Reference for the broader field context.
PINN review
[5]
Wang, S., Yu, X., Perdikaris, P. — When and why PINNs fail to train: A neural tangent kernel perspective
Journal of Computational Physics · vol. 449 · 2022
Explains PINN training failures through the lens of NTK theory. Motivates loss balancing strategies that improve convergence for stiff PDEs.
PINN training analysis
Thermal & Manufacturing Applications
[6]
MCO-PINN — Multi-constrained optimisation PINN for 3D transient temperature in large-scale aluminium alloy workpieces
Sensors · 2023 · Full 3D industrial quenching simulation (31 m workpiece). Provides architecture and training baseline for Level 8 of this thesis. No NAS component.
3D aluminium quenching
[7]
Physics-Informed Fully Convolutional Networks (PIFCN) for heat conduction in composite structures
ASME Journal of Computing and Information Science in Engineering · November 2024
CNN-based PINN outperforms MLP for spatially structured heat conduction. Highlights the architecture-problem fit question that motivates NAS.
CNN-PINN
[8]
Physics-informed CNN for heat source layout optimisation without labeled data
Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence · 2022 · arXiv:2109.12482
Demonstrates CNN-PINNs as surrogate models for thermal design optimisation — a direction complementary to this work.
CNN-PINN surrogate
[9]
Fjær, E.G. et al. — Distortion and residual stress in aluminium castings: FEM simulation and CMM validation
Light Metals · TMS Proceedings · 2004
Source of the 47-point CMM measurement benchmark used in Level 4 to validate PINN-predicted thermal distortions of A356 aluminium castings.
CMM benchmark
Neural Architecture Search
[10]
Bergstra, J., Bardenet, R., Bengio, Y., Kégl, B. — Algorithms for Hyper-Parameter Optimisation (Tree-structured Parzen Estimator)
Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) · 2011
Introduces the TPE algorithm used for Bayesian NAS in this thesis. TPE models p(x|y) and p(y) separately using kernel density estimators to guide search toward promising configurations.
Bayesian / TPE
[11]
Deb, K., Pratap, A., Agarwal, S., Meyarivan, T. — A fast and elitist multi-objective genetic algorithm: NSGA-II
IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation · vol. 6, no. 2 · pp. 182–197 · 2002
NSGA-II uses non-dominated sorting and crowding distance to simultaneously optimise L2 error and parameter count in the NAS search space.
NSGA-II
[12]
Deb, K., Jain, H. — An evolutionary many-objective optimisation algorithm using reference-point based non-dominated sorting: NSGA-III
IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation · vol. 18, no. 4 · pp. 577–601 · 2014
NSGA-III replaces crowding distance with reference-point diversity, intended for 3+ objectives. In this thesis it is used for the 2-objective (accuracy vs size) NAS problem.
NSGA-III
[13]
Elsken, T., Metzen, J.H., Hutter, F. — Neural architecture search: A survey
Journal of Machine Learning Research · vol. 20, no. 55 · pp. 1–21 · 2019
Comprehensive NAS survey covering search space definition, search strategies (Bayesian, evolutionary, RL), and performance estimation. Provides the framework used to categorise the three optimisers in this thesis.
NAS survey
Training Methodology
[14]
Glorot, X., Bengio, Y. — Understanding the difficulty of training deep feedforward neural networks
AISTATS · 2010
Introduces Xavier (Glorot) initialisation. All PINN models in this thesis use Xavier uniform initialisation to prevent vanishing/exploding gradients at the start of training.
Initialisation
[15]
Loshchilov, I., Hutter, F. — SGDR: Stochastic Gradient Descent with Warm Restarts
ICLR · 2017
Cosine annealing LR schedule with warm restarts. Used in Level 5 extended training (20,000 epochs) to escape local minima and achieve the L2=0.030 (Bayesian) and L2=0.055 (NSGA-II) results.
Cosine LR
Multi-Domain & Geometry Extensions
[16]
Tancik, M. et al. — Fourier Features Let Networks Learn High Frequency Functions in Low Dimensional Domains
NeurIPS · 2020
Random Fourier feature embeddings (mapping inputs to sinusoidal features) dramatically improve PINN accuracy for high-frequency solutions. Directly relevant to the Flower domain failure in Level 7 where the oscillating boundary cannot be represented by standard relu/tanh MLPs.
Fourier features
[17]
Sitzmann, V., Martel, J., Bergman, A., Lindell, D., Wetzstein, G. — Implicit Neural Representations with Periodic Activation Functions (SIREN)
NeurIPS · 2020
Sinusoidal activations throughout the network enable representation of high-frequency signal derivatives — superior to relu/tanh for PDE solutions with sharp gradients or oscillatory boundaries. Recommended as future extension for the Flower domain in Level 7.
SIREN
